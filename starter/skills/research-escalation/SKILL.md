# Research Escalation

Use Research when the problem may be larger than the symptom.

```text
contain urgent issue if necessary
→ symptom
→ workflow
→ reusable capability
→ platform / system
→ compare native + open-source + paid
→ Research / Watch / Test / Reject
→ test in the correct owner
→ promote proven reusable capability
```

Do not over-engineer one-off incidents.

A qualified Research result must state:

- problem being solved;
- broader capability hypothesis;
- alternatives compared;
- evidence and constraints;
- recommended test;
- owner of that test;
- promotion/rejection criteria.

Research is the proving ground. Production owners adopt only what earns promotion through evidence.
