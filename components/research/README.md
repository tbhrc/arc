# Research Component

Research is ARC's discovery and proving layer.

It receives external signals and recurring local capability gaps. Its purpose is not to accumulate links; it should produce decision-quality evidence and a clear next state:

```text
Reject | Watch | Research Further | Test | Adopt
```

Use the problem-to-platform lens:

```text
symptom -> workflow -> reusable capability -> platform/system
```

Compare native, open-source and paid options where material. Testing happens in the correct owner; Research retains the evidence and decision trail.
